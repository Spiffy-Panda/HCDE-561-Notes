# Work Context

## Work context

Panda is an HCI specialist with a background in VR and AI interfaces, currently enrolled in UW's Graduate Certificate in Human Centered AI (HCAI), taking courses including HCDE 560 and HCDE 561 (Strategic Human Centered AI). Panda is not a designer by training and is actively developing design language and methodology. Past professional experience includes prior industry work alongside graduate student interns on neural-interface research.

## Personal context

Panda lives in the PNW with a partner (Rattie) and keeps fancy rats as pets. Panda is vegetarian and has an active interest in cooking, food science, and DIY projects. Hobbies span a wide range: creative writing and worldbuilding, gaming (Skyrim modlists, Godot game development, tabletop RPGs), and hands-on technical tinkering including electronics, embedded systems, and spectroscopy. Panda prefers sustainability-oriented and cooperative/worker-owned purchasing options when available.
